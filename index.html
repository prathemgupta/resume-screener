How it works

Four simple stages, each easy to inspect.

🔎

1. Extract

Skills, education level and years of experience are pulled from each resume and from the job description.
🧮

2. Compare

TF-IDF vectors and cosine similarity measure how closely the wording of a resume matches the job.
📊

3. Score

Four sub-scores are combined with weights into one match percentage.
🏆

4. Rank

Candidates are sorted, with matched and missing skills listed for quick review.
